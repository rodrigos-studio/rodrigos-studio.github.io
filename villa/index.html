Questions

Before you pack

How do I pay the advance?

After you send the booking on WhatsApp we reply with our bank details. Send 30% and a photo of the slip, and the room is yours. The rest is paid when you leave, by cash or bank transfer.

Can I cancel?

Cancel 7 days before and we return the full advance. Closer than that, we move your dates once for free.

Is it cold at night?

Nights go down to about 14°C from December to February. Every bed has a thick blanket and the showers have hot water.

Do you have parking?

Yes, for two cars or one van, inside the gate.
