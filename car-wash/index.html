    Can I come without booking?

    Yes. Walk-ins are welcome, but booked cars go into the bay first, so a booking saves you the wait.

    Where do I wait?

    There's a shaded waiting area with free tea and Wi-Fi. You can watch your car being washed.

    Is the soap safe for my paint?

    We use pH-neutral foam and clean microfibre cloths for every car, so there are no scratches or swirl marks.

    How do I pay?

    After the wash, at the counter. Cash, card or bank transfer.
