Questions

Things owners ask me

Who owns the website?

You do. The web address is registered in your name, and I hand over everything. If you stop working with me, the site stays yours.

I don't use a computer. Is that a problem?

No. You send me changes on WhatsApp, like a new price or a photo, and I update the site.

How do customers order?

They tap items on your site, then one button sends the full order to your WhatsApp with their name and address. No app to install, no extra fees.

Can the site be in Sinhala?

Yes, Sinhala, English or both, with a button to switch.

What if I only want videos?

That's fine. I can film and edit short videos for your Facebook, Instagram or TikTok without building a website.
